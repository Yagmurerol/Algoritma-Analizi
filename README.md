🎬 Cinema Reservation & Management System
A desktop cinema ticketing, hall, and operational management system developed using C# and Windows Forms architecture. The project enables both cinema-goers to complete ticket and reservation processes and administrative staff to manage movies, screenings, and hall operations through a centralized interface.

📌 The Platform Includes
🎟 Ticketing & Reservation: Movie selection, screening schedules, and real-time seat booking through an interactive seat selection module (rYapcs.cs).

📋 Reservation Tracking: Detailed listing, querying, and status management for all booked tickets (Rezervasyonlar.cs).

🎞 Movie & Screening Management: Adding new movies to the catalog, updating movie details/posters, and managing active screening schedules (FilmEkle.cs).

👥 User & Staff Management: Customer registration and ticketing staff account administration (KullaniciEkle.cs, Kayıt.cs).

🔐 Admin Control Panel: Central control dashboard for monitoring hall occupancy, ticket sales, and system records (Admin.cs).
